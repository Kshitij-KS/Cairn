What this site is

Memory Atlas is an internal reference for one small team. It displays a summary of a private knowledge repository: note titles, how notes connect to each other, who last changed each one, and when. It is published so the team can look at it without a terminal.

What we collect from you

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. It keeps two small preferences in your browser's local storage, the theme you chose and when you last looked, which never leave your browser. It sends nothing about you anywhere.

What the site loads

  • A single generated file, data/graph.json, served from the same domain.
  • The D3 charting library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
  • One typeface from Google Fonts.

Those two providers receive your IP address and user agent as part of any ordinary web request, under their own privacy terms. Nothing else about you leaves this page. If you would rather not contact them at all, both files can be self-hosted; the change is two lines.

Server logs

The site is static. Your host (the template ships a vercel.json for Vercel) records ordinary request logs, including IP addresses, under its own policy. We do not add to those logs and do not use them. To confirm before publishing: the retention period on your hosting plan.

What the published data contains

Names of people on the team appear as authors of notes, alongside note titles, dates and a one line summary of observation-level notes. The contents of the notes are withheld unless a note is explicitly marked as safe to publish. Notes marked restricted are not included at all, and neither are the messages attached to changes. See the map and open How to read this for the current counts.

Removing something

The published file is generated from the repository. To remove an item, change its confidentiality marking in the repository and the next build drops it. There is no separate database to purge.
